# Aurum Vexillum Vault — visual identity

The interface uses a premium gold-and-black identity for dark mode and a white-and-gold identity for light mode.

- Dark background: `#080808`
- Light background: `#FBFAF6`
- Primary gold: `#D4AF37`
- Dark-mode gold: `#F0D98A`
- Light-mode gold: `#B8860B`
- Product mark: `AV` shield-inspired monogram
- Tagline: **Secure your WAX. Own your digital world.**

The visual system emphasizes restrained gold accents, high contrast, generous spacing, readable typography, and reduced-motion support. Avoid describing the product as audited or “military-grade” until independent security validation is complete.
