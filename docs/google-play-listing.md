## Full description

Secure your WAX. Own your digital world.

Aurum Vexillum Vault is a non-custodial wallet for the WAX blockchain. Manage public WAX account data, view tokens and NFTs, connect to supported dApps, and review blockchain transactions before you approve them.

### KEY FEATURES

• Non-custodial design
You control your recovery phrase and private keys. Aurum Vexillum Vault does not take custody of your funds and cannot recover a lost recovery phrase.

• WAX account management
View account permissions, token balances, transaction history, and public on-chain activity.

• NFT gallery
Browse NFTs held by your WAX account and view available public marketplace listing data where supported.

• Explicit transaction approvals
Review the network, account, permission, contract, action, recipient, quantity, memo, fee, and other transaction details before signing.

• dApp connections
Connect to supported WAX dApps with origin-bound sessions and revoke connections when you choose.

• Trading previews
Review token pairs, quotes, fees, slippage tolerance, minimum received amounts, and quote expiry before approving a trade.

• Professional interface
Use a premium gold-and-black dark theme or a white-and-gold light theme, with support for multiple languages.

### IMPORTANT SECURITY INFORMATION

Never share your recovery phrase or private keys. Verify every transaction before approval. Token, NFT, price, and marketplace information may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Marketplace listing prices are not guaranteed valuations.

Use test accounts until you have independently verified your recovery process and the wallet's security posture. This application is not a bank, custodian, broker, or guarantee of transaction success.

### NETWORK AND DATA

The app connects to public WAX blockchain, token, NFT, and marketplace services. Network availability and public data accuracy depend on those services. The app does not promise uninterrupted availability or a particular asset value.
