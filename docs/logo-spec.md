# Aurum Vexillum Vault — Logo Specification

## Primary emblem

The primary mark is a circular, gold-and-black mechanical vault emblem. It combines:

- A warm gold outer ring
- Concentric black and gold rings
- A central stylized vault door / shield form
- Mechanical gears and architectural linework
- Small metallic green-gold accent stones
- A premium, secure, technical character

The accompanying vector at `public/aurum-vexillum-vault-logo.svg` is a clean, scalable interpretation of the supplied reference image. It is intentionally simplified for reliable rendering at small sizes; it is not a pixel-for-pixel tracing of the raster artwork.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to at least 12.5% of the emblem width on every side. Do not place text, controls, or other marks inside this area.

## Minimum sizes

- App icon: 48 × 48 px minimum
- Browser favicon: 32 × 32 px minimum
- Header mark: 40 × 40 px minimum
- Print: 20 mm minimum diameter

At sizes below 64 px, use the simplified emblem without the smallest mechanical details if a dedicated compact asset is produced.

## Usage rules

- Use the emblem on obsidian, ivory, or transparent backgrounds.
- Preserve the original circular proportions.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add drop shadows, or place it on busy imagery.
- Keep the gold contrast strong enough for accessibility.
- Use the wordmark `Aurum Vexillum Vault` beside the emblem, never inside it.
- Do not imply WAX affiliation or endorsement without permission.

## Recommended lockups

**Primary:** emblem + `Aurum Vexillum Vault` wordmark

**Compact:** emblem only

**Social/avatar:** emblem centered on obsidian background

**Light UI:** emblem on ivory or transparent background

**Dark UI:** emblem on obsidian or transparent background
