# Aurum Vexillum Vault — Logo Specification

## Approved primary logo

`public/logo.png` is the approved primary emblem supplied for Aurum Vexillum Vault. It is a circular gold-and-black mechanical vault emblem with a central vault-door motif and green-gold metal accents.

Use the supplied PNG as the source of truth for the web header, favicon, README, social previews, and store artwork. The previous SVG is retained as a fallback vector concept only and must not replace the approved artwork.

## Usage

- Preserve the circular aspect ratio; never stretch or rotate the logo.
- Use on black, ivory, transparent, or uncluttered backgrounds.
- Maintain clear space of at least 12.5% of the logo width.
- Use the complete emblem at 48 px or larger where possible.
- Use a high-resolution source for app-store and native-app exports.
- Do not recolor, add filters, crop the emblem, or overlay text on the artwork.
- Confirm artwork ownership/licensing before public distribution.
